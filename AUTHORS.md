Version 2 research — survival targets, published-score benchmarks, landmark
cohort work, and multipage app changes — is designed, created, and maintained
solely by:

- **Pranjal Dubey** — [@pranjal-dubey-dev](https://github.com/pranjal-dubey-dev/)

did not clear its promotion bar; no landmark model is implemented or served.
Attribution for v2 should credit Pranjal Dubey. Please do not describe v2 as a
team-authored effort.
